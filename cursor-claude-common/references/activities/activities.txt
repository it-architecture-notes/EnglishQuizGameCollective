LIST 4:

1. Maintenance & Errands (Physical Labor)
The physical tasks required to keep the household, property, and people functioning.

Household Chores: Cooking, cleaning, laundry, taking out the trash.

Transportation: Commuting, school drop-offs, driving to practice.

Upkeep & Repairs: Car maintenance, yard work, seasonal home repairs.

Pet Care: Feeding, walking, grooming.

2. Management & Administration (Mental Labor)
The behind-the-scenes planning, scheduling, and financial work (often called the "mental load").

Finances: Paying bills, budgeting, filing taxes, managing insurance.

Logistics & Scheduling: Organizing the family calendar, booking doctor/dentist appointments, coordinating carpools.

Education: Helping with homework, communicating with teachers, signing permission slips.

3. Lifestyle & Connection (Social & Emotional)
The activities focused on relationships, personal growth, and relaxation.

Leisure: Movie nights, reading, pursuing hobbies, playing games.

Socializing: Hosting dinners, going out to eat, playdates, attending parties.

Events & Travel: Planning vacations, celebrating holidays, buying gifts, weekend road trips.


SEQUENCE OF ACTIVITIES
-----

Level 1: Daily Personal Routines (Simplest)
Why simple: Single-subject, repetitive, highly visual, strong verb-noun collocations (brush teeth, put on socks). No complex transactions or strangers.

Why simple: Familiar environment, predictable objects, basic verbs (clean, put, open, close). Still low social interaction.

Sweeping / vacuuming
